    Cillhub desktop

    What's new in 2.2.0

    • Full Tenor v2 live GIF integration, instant search & trending with high-resolution picker layout.
    • Comfortable Friends tab layout with spacious user cards, larger avatars, and enhanced hover styling.
    • Multi-user screen share split-grid, preview loop protection, and application audio stream controls.
    • Server Guide, media gallery channel grids, bookmark folders, and verified linked connections.

    2.1.0

    • Full native shell controls with hardware AV1 and H.264 encode diagnostics.
    • Voice audio DSP studio pipeline info: RNNoise, SpeexDSP, noise gate and high-pass filter.
    • Deadlock-free desktop auto-updater with 35-second safety recovery and instant status controls.

    2.0.0

    • Cillhub is open source. The app is a new installation: sign in once, then you can uninstall the old app.
    • Screen sharing on Windows can use the graphics card's AV1 encoder (recent NVIDIA, AMD and Intel GPUs).

    1.2.0

    • Cillhub is now a full program: the app no longer loads the website, it ships its own copy and starts faster. You stay signed in. New versions arrive as app updates; the "new version" notice in the app installs them.
    • Screen sharing on Windows can encode on your graphics card (H.264). You can turn this off under Screen Sharing.
    • Fixed: resizing or moving the window could make it stick and the mouse lag.

    1.1.0

    • Game activity: when you play a known game, others see "Playing …" next to your name. You can turn this off or add your own games under Activity Privacy.
    • Global keybinds: toggle mute (Ctrl+Shift+M) and deafen (Ctrl+Shift+D) even while Cillhub is in the background. Change them under Keybinds.
    • This settings window, opened from the tray menu with "Settings…".
